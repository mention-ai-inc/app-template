## Product design

Read `docs/product/design.md` before changing UI. Apply the agreed decisions relevant to the task; unresolved entries are not requirements. Product terminology lives in `docs/product/glossary.md`.

The scaffold's colors, fonts, component styling, copy, theme defaults, and feedback patterns are replaceable examples, not a prescribed brand. If the brief has no decisions yet, routine UI work can use the starter components without treating their appearance as a product choice. Use the `define-design` skill when the user wants to establish or revise that direction; do not require a design interview for every UI edit.

Preserve accessible controls, visible focus, readable content, and relevant loading, empty, error, pending, and disabled states when changing presentation. Choose copy, motion, layout, and feedback from the product's needs and agreed brief.
